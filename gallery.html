— Platter of the Month —

The perfect platter, built around crunch.

A great platter isn't cheese and crackers — it's balance. Soft cheeses, briny pickles, fresh fruit, something sweet, and a salty, crunchy backbone that won't let you down.

We love stacking our Pressed Pretzels next to triple-cream brie, pairing Plantain Chips with smoked almonds, and threading Twisted Pretzel Sticks right through the middle of a cured-meat rose. Pro tip: always leave more pretzels than you think you need.
