— Why we bake it this way —

Built around real ingredients and zero shortcuts.

Macy & Tailor ranges are consistently crafted without artificial flavours and without preservatives. We lean on oven baking, clean seasonings and quality base ingredients so the flavour on the label is the flavour in the bag.

Where we can go cleaner, we do — palm-oil free formats, dairy free variants and vegan options sit alongside the more indulgent flavours, so there's always something in reaching distance.
