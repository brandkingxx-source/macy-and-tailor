— Our Story —

Not your average snack. Not your average elephant.

Macy & Tailor started with a single pressed pretzel idea and a mascot who refused to be average. This is how a simple crunch became a snack shelf staple.

— Who we are —

Founded on flavour.
Built around community and crunch.

Macy & Tailor launched in 2017 with a simple, almost rebellious premise: that snacks could be tastier, cleaner and a little more fun than what was already sitting on New Zealand shelves.

The first star of the show was our pressed pretzel — a thinner, crispier, oven-baked take on the classic that worked just as well straight from the bag as it did stacked on a grazing board.

From there the range grew, but the spirit never changed. We still chase flavour first, cleaner ingredient decks whenever possible, and the kind of packaging you want to keep visible in the pantry.
